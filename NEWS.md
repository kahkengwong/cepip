# cepip 0.1.0

* First release: CEP-IP (GAM with PRSS k search, EP and TREP cells, inflection
  point with CI and IPRS), SKCS, the CMRF band and RSk-fCV gate, Validation1,
  the partner screen, per-donor group shares, donor-paired DEGs, donor-level
  association models, plots, and `cepip_save()`.
* `cepip_example()` downloads the example data from Hugging Face, and
  `cepip_example_clear()` deletes the cached files.
* Reproduces the SEA-AD PVM_3 and HKG8 arms of the CEP-IP with CMRF paper
  (`inst/scripts/reproduce_seaad.R`).
* Reproduces the PCa analysis of the published CEP-IP paper
  (`inst/scripts/reproduce_cmpb_pca.R`).
